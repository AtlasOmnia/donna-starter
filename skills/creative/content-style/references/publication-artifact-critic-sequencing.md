# Publication Artifact and Critic Sequencing

Use this when a sourced megathread draft also contains research notes, matrices, critic checklists, or other internal scaffolding.

## Required sequence

1. Preserve the full sourced dossier as a separate backup.
2. Extract and revise the actual public post into the canonical draft path.
3. Run deterministic checks before model review:
 - Canonically resolve every Reddit link and confirm the actual subreddit/title.
 - Check dates, placeholders, malformed tables, post size, and internal-only headings.
4. Dispatch the independent critic against the exact revised publication file—not the older research dossier.
5. Give the critic a narrow brief: current date, authoritative sources, exact file, and requested line-specific blockers/replacements.
6. Treat a context-exhausted critic as a failed audit, not a verdict. Re-dispatch on the smaller publication artifact.

## Why this matters

A visible `reddit.com/r/<expected-sub>/comments/<id>/` URL can canonically resolve to a different subreddit. The URL text alone is not proof. Also, sending a 50KB+ research bundle to a critic wastes context on internal notes and may produce no report.

## Deliverable discipline

- Keep research and publication artifacts separate.
- Report deterministic verification independently from model criticism.
- Do not claim the cross-model audit is complete until the critic returns an actionable report on the current file.
